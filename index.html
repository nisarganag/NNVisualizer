A LITTLE INTUITION GOES A LONG WAY

Understand every connection.

No black box. Just data, a few neurons, and a pattern waiting to be found.

01

Start with the data.

The dots are examples. In classification, their colors are the two categories your network is learning to separate. In regression, the goal is to predict a continuous value.

Training examples teach the network. Test examples show how it performs on data it has not trained on.

02

Follow the signal.

Each neuron combines its inputs using weights and a bias, then applies an activation function. Each layer transforms the signal a little further.

Training adjusts the weights to reduce prediction error. Hover over any neuron to see the pattern it has learned.

03

Read between the lines.

Mint means positive. Coral means negative. Thicker connections have larger weights. A neuron’s small dot represents its bias.

The output background shows predictions across the space. Color intensity shows the output’s magnitude, not calibrated probability.
