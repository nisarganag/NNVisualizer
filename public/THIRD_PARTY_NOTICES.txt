MNIST database of handwritten digits
------------------------------------
Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and Christopher J. C. Burges.
https://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
The Digits page's network was trained on MNIST (scripts/train-mnist-cnn.mjs),
and so was the Autoencoder page's (scripts/train-mnist-vae.mjs).
public/models/mnist-samples-v1.bin contains ten digits from the MNIST test set
and public/models/mnist-vae-examples-v1.bin one hundred; together with the map
positions of 5,000 test digits in public/models/mnist-vae-codes-v1.bin, they
are shared under the same license.


Project Gutenberg books (training data only)
--------------------------------------------
The Recurrent page's network was trained on nineteen public-domain books from
Project Gutenberg (https://www.gutenberg.org/):
  #11    Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, by Lewis Carroll
  #12    Through the Looking-Glass, and What Alice Found There, by Lewis Carroll
  #55    The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, by L. Frank Baum
  #54    The Marvelous Land of Oz, by L. Frank Baum
  #16    Peter Pan, by J. M. Barrie
  #45    Anne of Green Gables, by L. M. Montgomery
  #113   The Secret Garden, by Frances Hodgson Burnett
  #146   A Little Princess, by Frances Hodgson Burnett
  #120   Treasure Island, by Robert Louis Stevenson
  #236   The Jungle Book, by Rudyard Kipling
  #2781  Just So Stories, by Rudyard Kipling
  #271   Black Beauty, by Anna Sewell
  #289   The Wind in the Willows, by Kenneth Grahame
  #500   The Adventures of Pinocchio, by Carlo Collodi
  #708   The Princess and the Goblin, by George MacDonald
  #902   The Happy Prince and Other Tales, by Oscar Wilde
  #1597  Andersen's Fairy Tales, by Hans Christian Andersen
  #2591  Grimms' Fairy Tales, by Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm
  #1874  The Railway Children, by E. Nesbit
No Project Gutenberg file is distributed with Neural Network Visualizer. The
training script (scripts/train-char-lstm.mjs) reads the books from a local
download, removes the Project Gutenberg header, footer and license, and uses
the text only to train public/models/char-lstm-v1.bin and the gateless network
in public/models/char-rnn-v1.bin. Paragraphs containing
period slurs were left out of training. The page's example sentences quote
short passages from these public-domain works.
