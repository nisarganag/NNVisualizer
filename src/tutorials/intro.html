Start with a prediction

Each dot is an example. Its color is the answer we want the network to learn. The background shows the network's current predictions. Press Run training and watch the colors change as the weights and biases are adjusted.

Look inside a neuron

Hover over a circular neuron preview to see its output in the larger plot. Violet connections have positive weights; amber connections have negative weights. Thicker lines have larger magnitudes. Click or tap a connection or the small bias square to edit its value.

Try an experiment

Pause, switch between Circle and Spiral, and change the number of hidden layers. Compare training loss with test loss. Turn on Show test data to see examples that were not used to update the network. Copy the page's address to share the same starting configuration.
